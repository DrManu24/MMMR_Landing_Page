Medical Research.

Simplified.

We strive to provide end-to-end research services, working with clinicians from the study conceptualization stage (Study proposal/protocol writing, attaining scientific and ethical clearance) to Data Analysis (Data curation, cleaning and coding, basic to advanced statistical analysis) and finally, manuscript writing for publication (data visualization, multimedia editing, text formatting, grammar and reference check) in medical journal of choice.

About us

Manakkodam Manu Medical Research was founded to address a critical gap in our healthcare ecosystem: vast volumes of valuable scientific and clinical data generated across India remain unpublished, unanalyzed, and lost to medical science. We exist to unlock this potential by providing the methodological rigor, statistical expertise, and study design required to transform routine clinical observations and institutional data into high-impact, globally recognized evidence.

Our founder, Dr. Manu Pradeep (MBBS, MSc, MRCGP [INT.]), is a physician and clinical epidemiologist who combines active community practice with advanced academic research. After completing his core medical training at the Amrita School of Medicine, he earned his Master of Science in Epidemiology from the London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine (LSHTM), specializing in clinical trials, study design, and advanced statistical methods. He holds additional postgraduate qualifications in family medicine and pain management.

Dr. Pradeep has led and contributed to numerous randomized clinical trials and observational studies across rheumatology, clinical immunology, maternal health, and infectious disease, resulting in over twenty peer-reviewed publications in reputed international journals. Backed by extensive experience as a peer reviewer for leading global medical publications, he established Manakkodam Manu Medical Research to ensure that frontline clinical insights receive the rigorous epidemiological guidance needed to meaningfully advance modern medicine.
